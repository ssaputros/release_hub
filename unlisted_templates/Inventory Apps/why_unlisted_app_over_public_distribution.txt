We prefer unlisted app distribution over public distribution on the App Store because the app is designed for internal operational use by a specific organization and is not intended for the general public. Public listing could expose the app to unintended users, create confusion, and increase the risk of unauthorized access attempts.

Unlisted distribution allows us to keep the app discoverable only through a direct link shared with authorized employees and stakeholders. This provides a better balance between App Store availability and controlled access for the intended audience.
