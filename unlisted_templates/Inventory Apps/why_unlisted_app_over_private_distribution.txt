We prefer unlisted app distribution over private distribution via Apple Business Manager or Apple School Manager because the app is intended for a specific organization and needs a simple, flexible sharing method through a direct App Store link. This avoids the additional administrative setup required for private distribution while still keeping the app hidden from public App Store search and browsing.

For this use case, unlisted distribution provides a streamlined approach for authorized users to install the app without managing Apple Business Manager organization assignments or complex deployment configuration.
