My App addresses key business challenges related to inventory management, stock visibility, warehouse operations, and operational efficiency. It helps authorized staff manage inventory movements, review stock-related information, coordinate fulfillment activities, and access operational data needed for internal business processes. With sensitive company and operational data involved, the app prioritizes controlled access and is intended only for the organization’s authorized users.

Unlisted distribution supports these objectives by ensuring the app is accessible only to employees and authorized personnel through a direct link or internal channel. This avoids public discovery by unintended users, reduces confusion for the general public, and keeps distribution aligned with the app’s internal business use case.
